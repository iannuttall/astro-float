This post is an `.mdx` file. The paragraphs are ordinary Markdown and edit exactly like the other posts. Edited beside an island.

Float treats component blocks as **islands**. You can reorder them among the surrounding blocks (drag the grip, or use the arrows) and delete them after a confirm, but their MDX source is written back verbatim — Float never tries to turn rendered component HTML into Markdown.

<Callout title="This is a component" tone="tip">
  It's rendered from `<Callout>` in the source. Click it: the text won't take a caret, and a small bar appears with move up / move down / remove.
</Callout>
